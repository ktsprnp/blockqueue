Get in touch

Found a bug, have an idea or a question? The quickest way is from inside the app: open Settings and tap Send Feedback, or shake your device twice on any screen. Common questions

Where does the data come from?

Fees, blocks, transactions, addresses and prices come from mempool.space, a public Bitcoin explorer. Your device asks mempool.space directly, and Blockqueue has no server of its own. Blockqueue works with mempool.space® and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mempool.

Can Blockqueue send or receive bitcoin?

No. Blockqueue only shows public Bitcoin data. It is not a wallet, and it will never ask for your keys or seed phrase. If anything claiming to be Blockqueue asks for them, don't enter them.

What can I search for?

A transaction ID, a block hash or height, a Bitcoin address, a mempool.space link or a bitcoin: payment link. Your last 10 successful searches are kept on your device for each network. Press and hold an entry to remove it, or use Clear to remove them all.

The dashboard says it's reconnecting or isn't updating.

Live updates need an internet connection to mempool.space. Blockqueue reconnects on its own when the connection comes back, and pauses live updates while the app is in the background. If it stays stuck, check your connection or any VPN or content blocker, then close and reopen the app.

Why is there no price on Testnet4 or Signet?

Coins on test networks have no value, so amounts aren't converted to your currency there. Switch back to Mainnet in Settings to see prices.

What do sat/vB and sat/WU mean?

Both measure the fee rate a transaction pays. Most wallets use satoshis per virtual byte (sat/vB). One sat/vB equals 0.25 satoshis per weight unit (sat/WU). Pick the unit you prefer in Settings.

The app asks me to update before I can use it.

Very occasionally an older version stops working properly, for example when mempool.space changes how it sends data. Blockqueue then asks you to update. Tap Update on the App Store to open the App Store and install the latest version.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.
